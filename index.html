01 / THE IDEA

Logs move on.
Understanding should stay.

The next incident shouldn’t mean starting from zero. Logbook reads Oracle database logs and maintains a wiki that brings events, incidents, and database history together.

So the next time something looks familiar, there’s a place to start.
